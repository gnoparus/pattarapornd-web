# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective patients researching Dr. Pattarapornd before booking a consult, and secondarily a professional/referral network (other clinics, hospitals, colleagues) checking her credentials. Patient-first: the primary job on any visit is building enough trust and clarity to reach out or book, but the page must also hold up under a peer's credential check (experience timeline, training, specialties).

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio establishing Dr. Pattarapornd Suparcha's expertise across general practice, anti-aging/regenerative medicine, aesthetic medicine, and holistic wellness (Thai massage, sound healing). Success is a visitor leaving with clear understanding of her specialties and experience, and an easy path to contact her.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, approachable. Currently expressed through an Apple-inspired minimal purple palette and monochrome typography — the personality should read as a caring, credentialed practitioner, not a cold luxury brand or a clinical institution. Voice favors reassurance and clarity over hype.

## Anti-references

Not a salesy aesthetic-clinic ad: no overhyped before/after marketing, discount banners, countdown urgency, or pushy CTAs. Not a generic corporate hospital site either — avoid stock-photo clutter and impersonal tone. The warmth and credibility should come from real content (her actual journey, specialties, experience) rather than sales pressure.

## Design Principles

- Warmth over slickness — every section should read as personal and reassuring, not transactional.
- Credibility through specificity — real timeline, real credentials, real specialties beat generic trust badges.
- Calm confidence — minimal, spacious, Apple-inspired restraint; let content carry weight, not decoration.
- Patient-first, peer-proof — optimize primarily for a patient deciding to reach out, without sacrificing legibility for a professional scanning credentials.
- No sales pressure — clarity and information over urgency or persuasion tactics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline for a general audience: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation (relevant given the Three.js background and Framer Motion transitions already in use).
