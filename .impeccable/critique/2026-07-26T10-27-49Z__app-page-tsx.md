## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Start here: yes, a design-literate viewer clocks this as templated within the first two sections.**

**LLM assessment**: The hero-metric template appears verbatim (`10+ Years Experience / 6 Certifications / 55k+ Happy Patients`), and "55k+ Happy Patients" is internally implausible against her own listed timeline. Skills.tsx scores soft skills like "Patient-Centered Communication" at 98% — false numeric precision on an unmeasurable trait, a classic AI-resume tell, and it directly contradicts PRODUCT.md's "credibility through specificity." About, Services, Journey, Experience, and Skills all open with the identical eyebrow → centered bold headline → centered subhead scaffold (AchievementsVolunteering is the one exception, which reads as an inconsistency rather than intentional variation). MedicalSpecialties, Skills, and AchievementsVolunteering all repeat the same icon-chip/card grid shape. On the positive side: no `.gradient-text` or `.glass-effect` usage anywhere in components, no 01/02/03 numbered scaffolding, no literal text-overflow at either breakpoint tested. The individual absolute-ban patterns are mostly avoided — it's the macro-structure (same section skeleton × 7, decorative fake-precision stats) that reads as generated.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs --json app components` → exit 2, 3 static findings, all in `app/globals.css`: **gradient-text** (the `.gradient-text` utility itself, `#7c3aed`→`#a855f7`, line 194 — DESIGN.md explicitly prohibits this rule), **design-system-color** (`#a855f7` outside the documented palette, same rule), and **design-system-radius** (3px scrollbar-thumb radius outside the documented 8px scale — likely a low-value flag since a scrollbar thumb isn't a content component the design system was written to cover). Cross-referencing Assessment A's grep: the `.gradient-text` class is defined but never applied in any component — dead code, not an active violation, but it invites reuse of a banned pattern later and should be deleted.

**Visual overlays**: the injected detect.js overlay found 34–45 additional issues live in the browser (the banner count and per-line tally didn't reconcile — reporting both). Most consequential: **7 low-contrast instances**, white text (#fff) on near-white backgrounds (#f5f5f7/#fff) at ratios as low as 1.0–1.1:1 (need 4.5:1), hitting the hero H1, all three hero stat numerals, and headings in later cards — this reads like a real rendering bug (white text landing on a light section), not a design choice, and should be verified directly. Also flagged: 10 icon-tile-stack instances (icon-above-heading pattern across Specialties/Skills cards), 14 line-length violations (95–127 chars/line, target <80 — DESIGN.md itself specifies 65–75ch), 11 image-hover-transform instances, 1 clipped-overflow container, and 1 uppercase-body-text instance (the hero's tracked eyebrow line, which DESIGN.md's own Label rule permits — likely a detector false positive there given the project's documented exception).

## Overall Impression

The bones are good — real content, a disciplined single-accent palette, and a genuinely warm education/experience timeline. But two things undercut it: fabricated-feeling stats (55k+ patients, 98% skill scores) that fight the site's own "credibility through specificity" principle, and a Contact section that gives a convinced visitor nowhere to actually book — the single biggest opportunity is fixing the conversion path, since everything else is in service of getting someone there.

## What's Working

- **The Journey (education) timeline**: clean separator-based list, restrained "Current" pill in the one accent color, no unnecessary card chrome — best execution of "let content carry weight" in the whole site.
- **Mobile hero and mobile nav**: single clear CTA, legible text over a properly-scrimmed photo, clean slide-in menu with a sensible close affordance.
- **Photography and shadow system**: `shadow-apple-*`, rounded imagery, gradient overlays on card photos consistently deliver the Apple-inspired restraint the design system calls for.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] "Book Consultation" leads nowhere bookable.** Every primary CTA scrolls to Contact, which offers only LinkedIn, Facebook, and a static location card — no phone, email, form, or Line (the default contact channel in Thailand). This is the entire conversion goal of the site and it fails at peak intent. Fix: add a `tel:`/`mailto:`/Line link, or change the CTA copy to something honest if no booking channel exists yet. → `/impeccable clarify` or `/impeccable harden` (Contact section)

- **[P1] Fabricated-precision skill percentages.** Skills.tsx scores soft skills like "Patient-Centered Communication" at 98%. Contradicts PRODUCT.md's "credibility through specificity" and is one of the clearest AI-template tells on the page. Fix: drop the percentage bars; let description text carry the claim, or swap in a real verifiable metric. → `/impeccable distill` (Skills section)

- **[P1] Implausible hero stat.** "55k+ Happy Patients" beside "10+ Years Experience," for a doctor whose current listed role started this year. Exactly the "overhyped/salesy" pattern PRODUCT.md's anti-references ban, and it undermines trust in the first 3 seconds. Fix: replace with a specific, sourced number, or drop the stat row entirely. → `/impeccable clarify` (Hero section)

- **[P1] No visible keyboard focus state anywhere.** Confirmed live and by grep — zero `focus-visible` usage across all components. WCAG AA requirement, and PRODUCT.md explicitly commits to full keyboard navigation. DESIGN.md already specifies a 1px amethyst focus ring for buttons; it isn't implemented. Fix: add `focus-visible:ring-2 ring-[var(--apple-accent)]` to every interactive `<a>`/`<button>`. → `/impeccable audit` or `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] Low-contrast white text detected live (7 instances).** Detector overlay found white text at ~1.0–1.1:1 contrast on near-white backgrounds, hitting the hero H1, hero stat numerals, and later card headings — worth a direct visual check since this may be an active rendering bug rather than a false positive. Fix: verify against a live screenshot; if real, correct the background/text pairing at those specific elements. → `/impeccable audit`

- **[P2] Tonal mismatch in the Experience timeline.** The "Digital Commercial Artist" card uses a cartoon/anime-style image, styled identically to the clinical hospital photography around it. PRODUCT.md calls for the page to "hold up under a peer's credential check" — mixing a hobby side-gig in with equal visual weight reads as careless. Fix: de-emphasize or relocate this entry outside the main medical timeline. → `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Nav/IA inconsistency.** Header nav (Services/Education/Experience/Contact) and footer nav (`#home #services #about #contact`) expose two different, both-incomplete subsets of the site's 8 sections — About/Skills/Achievements are nav-orphaned in both places. Fix: reconcile both nav lists against the actual section set. → `/impeccable clarify`
